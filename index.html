Frequently asked questions

What is Abra Zylo AI SEO Generator?

It is an AI SEO generator for eCommerce that produces and validates search metadata, product descriptions, SEO URLs, and supporting product content for online stores.

Can Abra Zylo generate meta titles and descriptions?

Yes. As an AI meta title generator and AI meta description generator, the portal creates meta title and meta description fields and validates them against built-in quality checks.

Can Abra Zylo improve only failed SEO fields?

Yes. The platform supports field-level regeneration so only the sections that need attention are improved, without regenerating an entire product listing.

Does Abra Zylo support Groq, Gemini and OpenRouter?

Yes. The existing app supports provider selection through the generator and settings modules.

Does Abra Zylo include an eCommerce SEO audit tool?

Yes. The portal includes an SEO score checker for product pages, covering page title, meta description, focus keyword, content, and URL context.

Can Abra Zylo analyze Google PageSpeed Insights and Core Web Vitals?

Yes. The performance workflow acts as a Core Web Vitals checker using Lighthouse and Google PageSpeed Insights data.

Does Abra Zylo work as an OpenCart SEO generator?

Yes. Abra Zylo is built around OpenCart product workflows, making it a practical SEO content generator for online stores that need bulk product SEO without manual rewriting.

Can Abra Zylo generate SEO slugs and URLs in bulk?

Yes. The bulk product SEO generator workflow produces SEO-friendly slugs and URLs alongside meta titles, descriptions, and keywords for multiple products.
