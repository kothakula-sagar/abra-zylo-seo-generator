GETTING STARTED

Abra Zylo AI SEO Portal

One place to generate SEO content, audit pages, manage products, run sale campaigns, prepare the Meta catalog, and market to customers on WhatsApp — all for Abra Zylo.

What this app does

The portal takes a product image and a few details, and uses AI to generate a complete, scored SEO package for it: meta title, meta description, focus keywords, alt text, product description, slug, and tags. From there, the same product can flow into a Sale Campaign, the Meta Product Catalog, and WhatsApp marketing — so you do the SEO work once and reuse it everywhere.

The three data flows

  • SEO flow — Generate SEO → History (every generation is saved and reusable)
  • Marketing flow — Products → Sale Campaigns → Meta Product Catalog
  • Outreach flow — Customers → WhatsApp Marketing

All three are backed by the same Firebase project, so anything you add is visible to the rest of the team instantly.

GETTING STARTED

Typical Workflow

Here's the path most product SEO work follows, start to finish.

  1. Generate SEO — upload a product photo, add its name and category, generate a full SEO package, and save it. It now appears in History and in Products.
  2. Improve if needed — if the SEO score is low, use AI Improve on the weak field instead of regenerating everything.
  3. Add to a campaign — when a sale is coming up, create a Sale Campaign and add the relevant products to it.
  4. Move to Meta Catalog — once a campaign product's content is ready, it becomes available in the Meta Product Catalog for ad use.
  5. Tell customers — add or update customers in Customers, then send them a WhatsApp Marketing campaign about the sale.
  6. Check the site itself — periodically run a SEO Audit on live product pages to catch performance or on-page issues.

The Dashboard gives you a single view of where every one of these is at, and shortcuts to jump straight into any of them.

MAIN

Generate SEO

The core tool — turns a product photo and a few details into a complete, scored SEO package.

Required fields

  • Product image — JPG/PNG/WEBP, up to 10MB. Required — the AI uses it for context.
  • Product name — the exact name you want to appear in search results.
  • Category — helps the AI understand what kind of product this is.

Optional fields

Output language (English, Hindi, Telugu), AI provider (Groq, Gemini, or OpenRouter), and pricing fields (Model Number, MRP, Selling Price) — discount % and savings are calculated automatically for reference.

What you get back

Meta title, meta description, focus keywords, image alt text, product description, short description, SEO-friendly URL/slug, product tags, and an SEO Score out of 100.

AI Improve vs Full Regenerate

AI Improve regenerates just one weak field (e.g. only the meta title) and keeps everything else. Full Regenerate rebuilds the whole package — use it when the product info changed or the whole direction is wrong. Improvements are validated before they replace existing content, so a bad AI attempt never overwrites something usable.

Use + New Product in the top-right of the card to clear the form and start the next product without leaving the page.

History

Every SEO generation is saved here, so you never have to regenerate something that already exists.

  • Search by product name or filter by language.
  • Export JSON — download your generation history as a file.
  • Clear All — admin-only, wipes the saved history.

Check History before generating — the product may already have SEO content ready to reuse or improve.

MAIN

SEO Audit

Two tools in one page: a live site performance check, and a quick on-page SEO checklist.

Performance tab

Runs a real Google PageSpeed Insights / Lighthouse audit against any Abra Zylo page. Choose Mobile or Desktop, optionally turn on AI Performance Analysis for plain-English recommendations, and get back Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, and SEO scores plus Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS, FCP, Speed Index, TTFB). Requires a PageSpeed API key configured in Settings by an admin.

SEO Checker tab

A manual checklist — paste in a page title, meta description, focus keyword, content, and URL, and get an instant on-page SEO score. Useful for checking a page before it goes live, without needing an API key. Results can be saved to Audit History.

MAIN

Audit History

Every saved audit — performance or SEO checker — lives here so you can track a page's progress over time instead of only seeing the latest run.

AI MARKETING

Products

Every product that's been through SEO generation lives here, and can be reused across campaigns and the Meta catalog without re-entering its data.

Finding a product

  • Search by name or by who added it.
  • Sort: Newest, Oldest, Recently Updated, Name A–Z / Z–A.
  • Quick filters: Added by Me, Added Today, Updated Today.
  • Compare Selected — tick two or more products to compare them side by side.

Open a product from here to jump straight into it for a Sale Campaign or the Meta Catalog — no need to search again.

AI MARKETING · ADMIN ONLY

Sale Campaigns

Group products together for a promotional push — a festival sale, a clearance, a launch — and track each product's SEO/content status through it.

How it works

  1. Create a campaign with a name and a prompt describing the look/theme you want.
  2. Add products to it from your Products list.
  3. Each campaign item moves through Pending → Generating → Completed as its promotional content is produced.
  4. Once completed, campaign products become available for the Meta Product Catalog.

Campaign items track MRP, sale price, discount %, and savings alongside their SEO/content status.

AI MARKETING · ADMIN ONLY

Meta Product Catalog

The last step before products go into Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ads — organizes campaign-ready products by catalog status.

  • Browse by completed Sale Campaign, then filter products within it by All / Pending / Added.
  • Stat cards show Total, Pending, and Added counts for the open campaign at a glance.
  • Mark a product as Added once you've placed it in Meta Business Suite, so the team knows it's live.

WHATSAPP MARKETING

Customers

A shared customer directory the whole team adds to and uses for WhatsApp outreach.

  • Search by name or phone number.
  • Set each customer's status to Interested or Not Interested — this controls who gets included in WhatsApp campaigns.
  • Members and admins can add customers and change status; deleting a customer is admin-only.

WHATSAPP MARKETING

WhatsApp Marketing

Create a WhatsApp outreach campaign and open personalized, pre-filled messages for every subscribed (Interested) customer.

  • Create a campaign with a message template and an optional link.
  • Filter by gender if the campaign is targeted.
  • Open a campaign to see its recipient list, pulled from customers marked Interested.

Keep the Customers list up to date — WhatsApp campaigns only reach customers currently marked Interested.

ACCOUNT · ADMIN ONLY

Accounts

Where admins approve new sign-ups and manage who has access to the app.

  • New users appear as pending until an admin approves them — they can't use the app until then.
  • Approved users can be blocked if needed.
  • Shows each user's email and role.

ACCOUNT

Settings

Where you configure your own AI provider keys and preferences.

Sections

  • Groq API Key — free tier, the default AI provider used for generation.
  • Google Gemini API Key and OpenRouter API Key — alternate providers you can switch to per generation.
  • Google APIs (admin only) — the PageSpeed Insights key that powers the Performance tab in SEO Audit for the whole team.
  • Preferences — your default AI provider and other personal defaults.
  • Profile — your account details.
  • Firebase — connection status, useful for confirming the app is synced.
  • Data — export/manage your stored data.

Each provider key is independent — if Groq is rate-limited, switch the AI Provider dropdown on the Generate SEO page to Gemini or OpenRouter without losing anything.
